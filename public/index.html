FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Ask the chat assistant.

Do I need an appointment?

We recommend booking so we can prepare for your visit. You can book through the chat at any time, and our staff will confirm the slot.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring a valid ID and a list of any medicines you take. If you have records from another clinic, bring those too.

How much does a visit cost?

Prices for each service are listed above. Ask our team about payment options at the front desk.

I have pain or swelling. What should I do?

Please call the clinic straight away. If you have trouble breathing, heavy bleeding or swelling around the face or throat, go to the nearest emergency room. The chat assistant cannot give medical advice, and a staff member will be notified.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes. Tell us through the chat or call the clinic as early as you can so another patient can use the slot.
